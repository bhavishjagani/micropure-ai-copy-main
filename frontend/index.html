About MicroPure AI

MicroPure AI uses a YOLOv8 detector trained on a microplastic dataset to identify suspended particles in microscope images of water samples. Each detection is shaped, sized and area-weighted, then folded into a multi-factor water-risk score so a single low-confidence hit can never push the result to 100%.

Notes for clinicians & researchers

  • This tool is a research aid, not a regulatory measurement device.
  • Risk score is an internal index — calibrate against lab-verified ground truth before drawing conclusions.
  • Image quality (lighting, focus, magnification) significantly affects detection.
  • Higher inference image-size catches smaller particles but takes longer.
  • All processing happens on the local backend — no images are uploaded to a third party.
